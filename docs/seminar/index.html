02 · Motivation

One question

A neutrino is blueshifted as it falls toward a compact object. Its cross section grows. Does the geometry leave a fingerprint in what escapes?

Eloc = E∞ / √f(r)  →  σ grows  →  τ grows  →  how much?
√3
hard ceiling on the blueshift: every uncaptured ray has rt ≥ 1.5 rs, where f = 1/3
3α/2
therefore a hard ceiling on the cross-section ratio, set by the slope α of σ(E)
α
saturation flattens α at high energy — so the ceiling falls with energy
dα/dE
the signature is the derivative, not the value. A PDF refit shifts; it does not tilt.
This is the thesis of the talk in one slide. The ceiling is kinematic and exact. What makes it interesting is that it inherits the energy dependence of alpha, and saturation is precisely what bends alpha.

Below the grid, LHAPDF continues the PDF by a power law. That is a choice, not a measurement — and it is exactly the choice the dipole picture replaces.

The colour dipole

The boson fluctuates into a q q̄ pair of transverse size r; the pair scatters off the nucleon. Unitarity is built in: σdip saturates at large r instead of growing without bound.

The point is not that the collinear calculation is wrong. It is that above about 1e12 GeV it stops being a measurement of anything and becomes a continuation of a fit. We will quantify exactly that fraction later.
04 · Cross section

Five steps, one file at the end

ψT, ψLlight-cone wave functions; gV, gA per current
σdip(r,x)GBW and bCGC; saturation scale Qs(x)
FT, FLtabulated once in (x, Q²), bicubic
d²σ/dx dyone master formula, both currents
σ(E), dσ/dytables with nine metadata keys

Coupled by a file, not by a link

The dipole code and the transport code never compile together. The contract is a table with declared conventions — projectile, target, current, units — and a reader that refuses to compare two tables whose conventions differ.

Why that matters

A ν table and a ν̄ table are both valid cross sections, and their ratio is a perfectly computable number. Only the comparison stops meaning anything — and nothing inside either file can detect that.

The nine mandatory metadata keys are the one place where a wrong comparison would pass silently. That is why they are mandatory and why the reader throws instead of defaulting.

The two ingredients

Kutak & Kwieciński, EPJ C29 (2003) 521
ε² = z(1−z)Q² + z m² + (1−z) μ²
FT,L = Q²/4π² ∫d²r ∫dz |ψT,L|² σdip(r,x)

The r integral runs in ln r over [10⁻⁸, 10³] GeV⁻¹, and z is resolved logarithmically from both ends. A uniform grid misses the peak at r ~ 1/ε — which is 0.025 GeV⁻¹ at Q² = MW², against a step of 2.

GBW

σdip = σ0[1 − e−r²/4R0²(x)],   R0² = Q0−2(x/x0)λ

Four-flavour fit. The effective quark masses are part of the fit, not a free convention.

bCGC

Impact-parameter dependent, with the BFKL anomalous dimension and the Levin–Tuchin tail. A factor 10⁶ lived here: x0 = 0.00069, and 6.46·10⁻⁶ is the uncertainty, not a multiplier.

The x0 story is worth telling briefly: reading the exponent of the quoted uncertainty as a multiplier of the central value made the model give 0.10 of the HERA data it had been fitted to. The data caught it.

The electromagnetic limit of the same wave functions and the same dipole amplitude, against the reduced cross section. No refitting — the GBW parameters are the published ones.

The structure is visible

+15% at x < 10⁻⁴ and low Q², on the nose above x ≈ 3·10⁻⁴. That is what a two-parameter saturation model looks like against precision data — and saying so is more useful than quoting the mean alone.

10 · Method

How a factor 670 was found

y = 1 is an endpoint, not a forbidden point

A monotonicity test on the ceiling failed. Two measurements killed the easy explanations:

  • the noise scaled as N⁻¹, not N⁻⁴ — so it was not Simpson truncation;
  • doubling the F table from 121 to 241 nodes changed nothing — byte-identical runs.

N⁻¹ with the interpolation irrelevant has one cause: a discontinuity at a quadrature endpoint. The x loop starts at x = Q²/s, which is exactly y = 1 — a kinematic endpoint with a finite integrand, being zeroed.

noise in ln σα rises

before, 48 nodes/decade2.6·10⁻⁴26 of 78
after, 16 nodes/decade1.2·10⁻⁶0 of 78

It also removed a bias: σ rises 0.23%. And it is the third occurrence of one class — a hard boundary evaluated exactly at an integration endpoint. The two earlier ones were density support edges.

The transferable part is the diagnostic, not the bug: measuring the scaling exponent of the noise, and varying the interpolation independently of the quadrature, separated two candidate causes that otherwise look identical.

12 · Geometry

Killing the turning-point singularity on paper

before any floating point
w(r) = 1 − f(r) b²/r²   (vanishes at rt)
T(r) = [ f(rt) r² − f(r) rt² ] / (r − rt)
with r = rt + s²:   dl/ds = 2r √( h f(rt) / T(r) )

What cancels

The Jacobian factor s cancels the √(r−rt) analytically. The integrable singularity never reaches the arithmetic.

What survives, correctly

A logarithmic divergence as b → bcrit⁺. That one is physical: the ray winds on the photon sphere and the proper length really does grow without bound. The substitution killed the square root, not the logarithm.

Distinguish the two divergences clearly. One is an artefact of the coordinate and must be removed exactly; the other is physics and must be preserved.

Emitted rays: four cases, not three

b = remit sin ψ / √f(remit)

Because g(r) = r/√f has a minimum at the photon sphere, the escape condition inverts below it. Above rph, outward always escapes; below it, outward with b > bcrit is captured — it rises, turns above the emission point, and falls back.

sin ψc = bcrit √f(r) / r,   fcap = (1 − cos ψc)/2
remitψccaptured fractionmeasured
1.5 rsπ/21/2rel 0

Isotropic emission in the local frame is cos ψ uniform, not ψ uniform. A Monte Carlo of 10⁶ samples agrees with the analytic fraction to 0.01σ.

A regime the original specification missed: emitted outward with b < bcrit, the turning point does not exist — not virtual, absent. But w never approaches zero there, so there is nothing to factor and nothing to cancel.

The inverted case below the photon sphere is the one that is easy to get wrong, because the intuition from rays coming in from infinity points the other way.

The curves cross the reference's constant α = 0.363 near 10⁷ GeV and keep falling to 0.24 at 10¹⁴. The power-law fit is only valid at the start of its own stated range.

16 · Result

Measure the shape, not the normalisation

invariant to 2·10⁻¹³
R(b,E) = (τfull − τfrozen) / τfrozen = ⟨ f−α/2 ⟩ − 1

Same geometry, same density. Only the argument of σ changes: Eloc(r) against E∞.

  • the dipole suppresses σ globally and flattens α;
  • comparing τ or P mixes the two, and the first dominates;
  • R is a weighted average of a ratio — a global factor k cancels.

Measured

Exactly 0 for k ∈ {0.5, 2} — powers of two, the rescaling is exact in binary — and ≤ 2.6·10⁻¹⁶ for k = 10. Worst of 27 combinations: 2.1·10⁻¹³.

Why it is exact

The adaptive quadrature uses a purely relative tolerance. Scaling the integrand scales the estimate, the tolerance and the error estimate alike — so the subdivision pattern is identical, not merely similar.

The switch that makes this possible was built for a different reason, two phases earlier. That is worth admitting rather than presenting as foresight.

The freeze-redshift switch existed to test whether the redshift mattered at all. It turned out to be the instrument that isolates shape from normalisation. Say plainly that this was not planned.

Magnitude, honestly

2 to 4 percentage points — not the 4.3 estimated a priori from 30.18 against 30.15.

What that costs

3 points in σ is Δτ = 0.15 at τ = 5, or 16% in P. Detectable in the calculation. Whether it is detectable in a telescope is a different question, and the answer belongs in the paper.

Measured directly at E = 1.09·10⁷ GeV, b → bcrit⁺: R = 0.1914 (dipole) against 0.2237 (collinear).

Do not oversell. The effect is a few percent and the observability question is genuinely open. Saying so is what makes the rest credible.
19 · Result

Regeneration: a kernel that has to depend on energy

2.2·10⁻¹⁶ at and between nodes
Kij(E) = σNC(E) Gij(E),   Σi Gij + Gleak,j = 1

y is invariant under redshift — initial and final neutrino sit at the same r, the same √f appears in both. So the y intervals linking bin j to bin i never move; only the argument of dσ/dy does.

But the real table's y-shape varies by a factor 40 between 10⁵ and 10¹¹ GeV. A frozen Gij is not an approximation, it is an error.

Why linear interpolation, specifically

The consistency identity is linear in the G. Linear interpolation of a set that sums to 1 at every node still sums to 1 between nodes. Number conservation is exact at every energy, not just at the tabulated ones.

With a cubic it would fail — and the failure would appear as flux created or destroyed along the ray, which nothing downstream would catch.

End to end, with CC absorption switched off: Σφ + leakage conserves to 4.4·10⁻¹⁶. With it on, regeneration raises the surviving flux by +4.5%.

This is the piece the code had explicitly deferred as "Phase 4b". The linearity argument is the reason the implementation is short and the conservation is exact rather than approximate.
20 · Honesty

What this does not do

measured, not guessed

The collinear reference extrapolates

Fraction of σ coming from x below the PDF grid: 0% up to 2.3·10¹⁰ GeV, 0.5% at 1.9·10¹¹, 4.3% at 1.6·10¹², 20.7% at 1.3·10¹³. Above that, its α is a property of the continuation, not a measurement.

Emitted rays + non-spherical profile

The ODE route never received the emission topology. For an emitted ray it is wrong by a factor 7 in one case, throws in another, and returns τ = 0 in silence for the radial one. Rays from infinity with a spherical profile are validated; the rest is not.

The emitter is static

Aberration between the fluid and static frames is the larger of the two effects: β = 1/2 exactly at the ISCO, so the Doppler factor sweeps 1/√3 to √3 — a factor 3 in energy, against the √3 ceiling of the gravitational redshift. Deferring it is sequencing, not a claim that it is small.

Putting the ODE gap on a slide at a collaboration meeting is deliberate. It is the honest state of the code, and someone in the room may be about to make the same mistake.

What the tests are actually for

247 checks · 8 phases · 0 failures
  • Every acceptance test has a reference value, not a golden file — an analytic limit, a published number, or an exact identity.
  • Convergence is tested by order, not tolerance: Zdiscrete converges at order 2.00, and that is the assertion.
  • A result that missed its tolerance is flagged, never delivered as if it had not.
  • Silent defaults on physics parameters were removed — one of them produced σNC/σCC = 2.49 instead of 0.42, smoothly and without an exception.
